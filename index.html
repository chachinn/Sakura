Special sounds: っ / ッ and ー
っ / ッ — small tsu (sokuon)

It usually does not make a full “tsu” sound. It creates a short pause and doubles the next consonant.

きて → kite
きって → kitte
がっこう → gakkou
バッグ → baggu
こっち → kocchi
ー — long vowel mark (chōonpu)

Common in Katakana, it lengthens the preceding vowel. Sakura writes the longer sound by repeating that vowel.

ケーキ → keeki
コーヒー → koohii
スーパー → suupaa
タクシー → takushii
